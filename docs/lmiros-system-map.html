What each sidebar item does

In plain English — and where each one gets its data.

📊

Overview

Source: all tables combined

Your home screen. Headline numbers — total leads, how many became sales, total revenue, ROAS — at a glance.

Pulls from: Leads + Sales records + Ad spend.

Leads

Source: Zoho Sheet (auto every 10 min)

The full list of everyone who submitted a form. Click one to see their details, contact history and status.

Where the data comes from: pulled from your Zoho spreadsheet automatically. No manual entry.

Sales

Source: Telegram bot + Zoho outcomes

Every closed deal and the revenue it brought. This is what feeds the "real money" side of ROAS.

Where the data comes from: mostly the Telegram /sale bot now; also any "Approved/Closed + Deal Amount" rows in Zoho.

Unmatched sales

Source: Sales records with no lead

A safety net. If a reported sale's phone number doesn't match any lead, it lands here so nothing is silently lost — you can match it by hand.

Where the data comes from: sales whose phone didn't line up with a known lead.
♻️

Rejected pool

Source: Rejected leads table

Rejected leads aren't deleted — they're saved here as an asset you can recycle later or resell. Filter by "Recycle eligible" or "Resale eligible". (Full breakdown below.)

Where the data comes from: filled automatically during the Zoho sync based on eligibility + agent outcome.
📈

Real ROAS

Source: Sales ÷ Ad spend

The core question answered: which websites and agents actually make money? Revenue divided by ad spend. Switch the "by" filter to Website, Agent, Loan type, etc.

Rejected Pool — the full story

A rejected lead is never thrown away. It's stored as a reusable asset with a reason and a "what to do next" flag.

Where they're stored & how they get there

During the every-10-minute Zoho sync, LMIROS looks at each lead and decides if it's a rejection. If yes, a copy (with a snapshot of loan type, region, source) is saved into the Rejected pool. It's separate from the live Leads list so your funnel stays clean, but nothing is lost.

Two kinds of rejection

TypeWho decidesMeaningWhat happens to it
Type 1 — Not Eligible The system, automatically from the form The lead can never qualify for this product — known without any follow-up. RESALE Marked to sell to another lender (it's a valid person, just not for you).
Type 2 — Rejected after follow-up The agent, after WhatsApp contact Eligible lead, but didn't proceed (not suitable now, or couldn't be reached). RECYCLE Kept to re-contact later — circumstances may change.

The rejection reasons explained

ReasonPlain meaningTypeNext action
out_of_coverageLives outside your service areas (only KL, Selangor, Seremban, Putrajaya are covered).1 · autoresale
wrong_loan_typeAsked for a loan you don't offer (anything not a Personal Loan).1 · autoresale
not_eligibleForm shows a disqualifier — e.g. self-employed/freelance, or salary paid in cash (not bank).1 · autoresale
not_suitableAgent spoke to them and decided it's not a fit right now.2 · agentrecycle
unreachableAgent tried but couldn't get hold of them.2 · agentrecycle
📌 On the Rejected Pool page you'll see small count tiles per reason, and three tabs: All, Recycle eligible (re-contact later), and Resale eligible (sell on). Open any row to action it.

Where the system can improve or adjust

Ordered by value-for-effort. Green = do this for biggest payoff.

⭐ Highest value · not code

Get every agent using /sale

The bot is live, but it only works if agents pair once (/start their-email) and report deals. This single habit turns "monthly guess" into "live truth" and makes ROAS finally accurate.

Effort: tiny (training) · Payoff: huge
⭐ High value

Capture outcomes for the back-catalogue

~1,000 past leads have no recorded result. A short push to log historical wins (even rough totals) would instantly enrich every chart.

Effort: low–medium · Payoff: high
Improvement

Per-campaign ROAS (not just per-website)

Today you see which website earns. To see which Google Ads campaign/keyword earns, the website form needs to capture the campaign tag (UTM/gclid) into Zoho. Then LMIROS can split ROAS by campaign.

Effort: medium (needs form change) · Payoff: sharper ad decisions
Improvement

Upload ad spend more often / automatically

Spend is uploaded by hand. A weekly habit keeps ROAS honest; later, a direct Google Ads connection could automate it.

Effort: low now / medium to automate · Payoff: always-fresh ROAS
Launch hardening

Security & always-on before go-live

Rotate the passwords/tokens that appeared during setup, lock data access per team, and move the live pipeline to 24/7 hosting. Safe to defer while testing.

Effort: medium · Payoff: production-ready
Nice-to-have

Clearer dashboard time ranges

Add obvious "All-time / This month / Last 7 days" toggles so numbers like "294 vs 1,027" are never confusing — they're just different time windows.

Effort: low · Payoff: clarity
